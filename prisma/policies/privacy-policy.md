## The principles we follow

Act 843 sets out principles for handling personal information. We apply them as follows.

- **Accountability:** the operator named above is responsible for how your information is handled.
- **Lawfulness of processing:** we only use information where there is a lawful ground, such as carrying out your booking, meeting a legal obligation or your consent.
- **Specification of purpose:** we collect information for the specific purposes listed in this policy.
- **Compatibility of further processing:** we do not go on to use information for an unrelated purpose.
- **Quality of information:** we aim to keep information accurate and up to date, and you can ask us to correct it.
- **Openness:** this policy tells you what we collect and why.
- **Data security safeguards:** we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information.
- **Data subject participation:** you can exercise the rights described below.

We also keep to data minimisation: we collect what the service needs, not what might one day be useful.

## Payments

Online payments are processed by Paystack. When you pay, your card, bank or Mobile Money details are entered with Paystack and handled on Paystack's systems. The studio does not receive or store your full card number, card security code or Mobile Money PIN. Your use of Paystack is subject to Paystack's own terms and privacy practices.

We keep only what we need to match a payment to a booking:

- payment reference;
- transaction status;
- amount and currency;
- payment date;
- the payment channel reported by Paystack;
- the booking reference;
- the transaction record Paystack returns to us, which may include limited details such as the card type and the last four digits of a card.

## Student verification

Student pricing is available only to eligible KNUST students, so we need to confirm student status. Depending on the method the studio has chosen, we may ask for:

- a KNUST student email address;
- a student ID reference;
- your student status;
- other information reasonably needed to confirm eligibility.

We use this information only to decide whether student pricing applies and to prevent misuse of it.

**We will never ask for your KNUST portal password or any other KNUST login details.** No part of this website requires them. If anyone claiming to act for the studio asks for them, do not provide them, and tell us.

## How we protect information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, with the aim of keeping it confidential, accurate and available to those who need it. These include:

- access controls and restricted administrative access;
- secure sign-in, with passwords stored in hashed form;
- encrypted connections (HTTPS);
- payment handled through Paystack's secure integration;
- checks on the server for information submitted through the website;
- logging of administrative actions;

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us using the privacy contact above.
