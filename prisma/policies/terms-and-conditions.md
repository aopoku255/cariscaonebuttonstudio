## Memberships

A membership is a prepaid allowance of studio hours for a fixed period.

- **Activation:** a membership becomes active once payment for it has been received and verified.
- **Hours:** the number of hours included and the period they are valid for are shown on the membership before you buy it.
- **Using hours:** hours are drawn down as you book. Each booking remains subject to availability and to the studio policies.
- **Unused hours:** hours that are not used before the membership expires are lost, unless the membership states otherwise.
- **Additional hours:** hours beyond the allowance are charged at the rate shown for your membership at the time of booking.
- **Renewal:** memberships do not renew automatically unless you have agreed to that with the studio.
- **Transfer:** a membership is personal to the member. It cannot be transferred to, or shared with, another person unless the studio has agreed in writing.
- **Student memberships:** only eligible KNUST students may hold a student membership. See the [Student Booking Policy](/student-policy).
- **Suspension and cancellation:** we may suspend or end a membership that has been obtained with false information, shared in breach of these terms, or used in serious breach of the studio policies. Refunds for a cancelled membership follow the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).
