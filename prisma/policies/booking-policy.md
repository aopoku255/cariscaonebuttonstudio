## How to book

1. Choose a package.
2. Pick a date and an available start time.
3. Add any production services you need.
4. Enter your details.
5. Review the booking, accept the studio's terms and policies, and pay.

You can book as a guest or with an account.

## When a booking is confirmed

A booking made online is confirmed when payment has been received and verified, unless an administrator has approved a different arrangement. Where the studio has switched on booking approval, a paid booking is also subject to that approval.

While you are paying, the slot is held for {{booking.pendingExpiryMinutes}} minutes. If payment is not completed in that time, the slot is released and may be booked by someone else.

## Available hours

Sessions can be booked within the studio's opening hours, which are published on this website. Opening hours can change, and individual dates or times may be unavailable because of University events, maintenance or other bookings.

## Late arrival

Your session runs for the time you booked. If you arrive late, the session still ends at the booked finish time, so that the next booking can start on time.

Late arrival rule: {{booking.lateArrivalRule}}

## Early departure

If you finish early, the remaining time is not refunded or carried over.

## Overtime

A session must end at the booked finish time, including the time needed to pack up. Extra time is only possible if the studio is free and staff agree to it.

Overtime rule: {{booking.overtimeRule}}

If you do not attend and have not cancelled, the booking is treated as a no-show. The no-show rule is shown in the list above.

## If the studio closes or cancels

The studio may need to close, or to withdraw a slot, because of KNUST events, maintenance, security concerns, power or infrastructure problems, emergencies or institutional requirements.

If the studio cancels a confirmed booking, you will be offered a reschedule or a refund as set out in the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).

## Emergency closure

In an emergency the studio may close without notice, and a session in progress may be stopped. Follow the instructions of staff and of KNUST security. Sessions lost to an emergency closure are treated as cancelled by the studio.

## Memberships and corporate bookings

Bookings made with membership hours follow this policy, with the membership rules in the [Terms and Conditions](/terms-and-conditions). Corporate bookings may be covered by a separate contract, which takes priority for that engagement.
