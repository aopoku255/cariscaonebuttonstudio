This policy explains when {{studio.name}} gives a refund and how it is paid.

## When a refund is available

A refund is available:

- when you cancel within the time allowed for a refund (see below);
- when the studio cancels a confirmed booking and you choose a refund instead of a reschedule;
- when a payment succeeds but the booking cannot be confirmed;
- when you have been charged in error;
- in any other case where the law requires one.

## When a refund is not available

A refund is not normally given:

- for a cancellation made after the time allowed for a refund, except to the extent shown above;
- for time not used because you arrived late or left early;
- where a session is ended because of a serious breach of the [Studio Policy](/studio-policy) or the [Content and Recording Policy](/content-policy);
- where a booking was obtained with false information, including a false claim to student pricing.

This does not affect any right to a refund that the law gives you.

## If the studio cancels

If the studio cancels a confirmed booking, or cannot provide the session because of a closure, you may choose between a reschedule at no extra cost and a full refund of the amount paid for that booking.

## If a payment succeeds but the booking is not confirmed

Occasionally a payment may go through although the booking cannot be confirmed, for example because the slot was released before the payment was verified. If that happens we will confirm the booking for the slot where it is still free, or offer you an alternative, or refund the payment in full. The choice between an alternative and a refund is yours.

## How refunds are processed

Refunds are returned to the original payment method through the payment provider. They are not paid in cash or to a different account, unless the original method cannot be used and the studio agrees an alternative with you.
